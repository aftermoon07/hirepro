hirepro : An AI-based Job Portal where Job posters can post their jobs and Jobseekers can apply for jobs. It is made using Next.js , Express , Node , Tailwind, Mistral 7B Gen AI LLM and Auth0. Five major functionalities are embedded :- Job Creation, Job Search,Job Management, Review System, mock Interview practice with 15+ minor functionalities as well. Various subcategories like Search Jobs , Filter Jobs , view my Jobs , view my posted Liked Jobs, view my liked Jobs , view reviews , etc. Also Implemented an AI-based Interview System that automatically generates interview questions and evaluates responses for over 100 different job roles, reducing hiring time.
